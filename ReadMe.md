# ServeNest

ServeNest is an application designed to provide household services on demand. Whether you need cleaning, plumbing, electrical work, or any other household service, ServeNest connects you with trusted professionals in your area.

## Features

- **User-Friendly Interface**: Easily navigate the app to find the services you need.
- **Verified Professionals**: All service providers are thoroughly vetted to ensure quality and reliability.
- **Real-Time Tracking**: Monitor the status of your service request in real-time.
- **Secure Payments**: Convenient and secure payment options for all services rendered.

## Getting Started

1. **Download the App**: Available on both iOS and Android platforms.
2. **Create an Account**: Sign up with your email or social media accounts.
3. **Request a Service**: Choose the service you need and provide details about your requirements.
4. **Connect with Professionals**: Receive quotes from available service providers in your area.
5. **Schedule and Pay**: Choose a convenient time for the service and make secure payments through the app.

## Conclusion

ServeNest aims to simplify the process of finding and hiring household service professionals. With a focus on quality, convenience, and user satisfaction, we strive to be your go-to solution for all your household service needs.
